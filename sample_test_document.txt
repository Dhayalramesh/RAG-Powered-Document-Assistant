1. COMPANY OVERVIEW
===========================================================

NexaCore Technologies Pvt. Ltd. was founded in 2014 and is
headquartered in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India. The company
specializes in enterprise software solutions, cloud
infrastructure management, and AI-driven analytics platforms.

As of the most recent fiscal year, NexaCore employs 1,240
people across four offices located in Coimbatore, Bengaluru,
Pune, and a satellite office in Singapore.

The company's mission statement is: "To simplify enterprise
technology through intelligent automation and human-centered
design."

===========================================================
2. PRODUCT LINES
===========================================================

2.1 NexaCloud
NexaCloud is the company's flagship cloud infrastructure
management platform. It supports multi-cloud deployments
across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud. NexaCloud was launched
in 2017 and currently serves over 3,500 enterprise clients.

2.2 NexaAnalytics
NexaAnalytics is a business intelligence and predictive
analytics tool. It uses machine learning models to forecast
demand, detect anomalies, and generate automated reports.
The product was launched in 2020 and has a customer
satisfaction score of 4.6 out of 5.

2.3 NexaAssist
NexaAssist is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed for
enterprise helpdesk automation. It integrates with Slack,
Microsoft Teams, and email systems. NexaAssist reduces
average helpdesk resolution time by approximately 38%.

3. HUMAN RESOURCES POLICIES
===========================================================

3.1 Leave Policy
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual
leave, 12 days of sick leave, and 8 days of casual leave per
calendar year. Unused annual leave (up to 10 days) can be
carried forward to the next year.

3.2 Work Hours
Standard working hours are from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday
through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break. Employees may
opt into a flexible hours program after completing six
months of employment.

3.3 Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject
to manager approval. Fully remote arrangements require
approval from the Head of Department and HR.

3.4 Probation Period
New employees undergo a probation period of 90 days, during
which either party may terminate employment with 7 days'
notice.

5. CUSTOMER SUPPORT GUIDELINES
===========================================================

5.1 Response Time SLA
- Critical issues: response within 1 hour, resolution
  target within 8 hours.
- High priority issues: response within 4 hours, resolution
  target within 24 hours.
- Standard issues: response within 1 business day.

5.2 Escalation Process
If an issue remains unresolved beyond the SLA window, it is
automatically escalated to a Level 2 support engineer. Issues
unresolved after 48 hours are escalated to the Engineering
Manager on call.

5.3 Customer Feedback
Customer feedback is collected via quarterly surveys. The
most recent survey (Q1 2026) recorded an overall satisfaction
score of 88%, up from 84% in the previous quarter.

6. SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE
===========================================================

NexaCore is ISO 27001 certified and compliant with GDPR for
its European clients. All customer data is encrypted at rest
using AES-256 encryption and in transit using TLS 1.3.

Security audits are conducted twice a year by an independent
third-party firm. The most recent audit, completed in
January 2026, identified zero critical vulnerabilities.
